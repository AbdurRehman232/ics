About ICS

Formulated for the mill floor, not just the lab bench

ICS manufactures and supplies textile processing chemicals for pretreatment, dyeing, printing, and finishing — built for consistency at production scale, not just laboratory conditions. Our in-house technical team works alongside mills to troubleshoot process issues, validate recipes, and adapt formulations to local water and fabric conditions.

Every batch runs through quality control before it leaves the facility, and our export desk handles documentation, MSDS/TDS packs, and logistics for international buyers from first inquiry to delivered container.
